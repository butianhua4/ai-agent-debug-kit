- The automation stopped running.

- The team does not know whether the issue is prompt, data, API, code, or config.

## FAQ

### Can you fix it without my password?

Yes for diagnosis and many rescue plans. I work from sanitized logs,
screenshots, public links, and redacted workflow exports. If implementation
requires privileged access, I will stop and describe exactly what your own team
needs to do.

### Can you guarantee the workflow will be fixed?

No. I can guarantee a scoped diagnosis, evidence-based rescue plan, and clear
handoff. Implementation success depends on the system, access limits, third
party APIs, and what materials are safely available.

### How fast can this start?

The diagnostic path can start as soon as the client sends safe material:
platform/tool name, screenshot, sanitized log, expected result, actual result,
and deadline.

### What if the issue is too risky?

I will refuse or re-scope if the request requires secrets, regulated data,
payment access, private account bypassing, or unsafe platform behavior.
