# Visual Asset Production Checklist

Purpose: turn the current offer copy, report templates, and demo proof into upload-ready Fiverr and domestic-platform assets without changing or deleting existing gigs.

Use this when preparing gallery images, PDF attachments, short videos, or social cover cards for the AI automation rescue / 299 RMB diagnosis offer.

## Asset Set

Create assets in this order:

1. Cover image: stops the scroll and explains the offer in one glance.
2. Proof image: shows the sample diagnosis report or failure map.
3. Scope image: explains what buyers should send and what not to send.
4. Price ladder image: 299 / 999 / 1999+ package split.
5. One-page PDF: gives buyers a polished service overview.
6. Short video/GIF: shows before/after workflow diagnosis.

## Fiverr Gallery

Recommended minimum:

- 1 primary cover image.
- 1 proof/report screenshot.
- 1 PDF one-pager.

Optional:

- 1 short video or GIF if upload is smooth.

Fiverr image guidance:

- Use clear English for Fiverr.
- Avoid tiny text.
- Use the same name/profile tone as the live gig.
- Do not promise guaranteed fixes.
- Do not show fake client orders or fake reviews.
- Do not include private keys, customer names, or platform dashboards.

## Quality Checklist

Before uploading:

- Text is readable on phone.
- One asset has one main message.
- No fake reviews, fake orders, or fake client names.
- No sensitive data appears.
- Price matches the current service ladder.
- Delivery boundaries are visible.
- The proof asset links to a real public sample or template.
- The file opens locally.

## Upload Decision

Upload without changing old gigs only when:

- It is a gallery/image/PDF addition.
- No deletion or pause is required.
- No payment, subscription, KYC, tax, payout, or wallet step appears.
- The user has provided any required manual upload if browser upload is blocked.

Stop and ask the owner when:

- Fiverr asks for identity, tax, payout, subscription, or payment.
- A buyer asks to move off-platform.
- A buyer sends sensitive secrets.
- A platform asks for private repo/OAuth/high-permission access.
