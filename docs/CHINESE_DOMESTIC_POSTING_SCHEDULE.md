# Chinese Domestic Posting Schedule

Use this schedule to promote the RMB 299 AI automation diagnosis offer across domestic channels without spam, fake scarcity, private-data requests, or unsafe platform behavior.

The goal is not to blast every platform. The goal is to create repeated, buyer-safe exposure and route interested people into the same intake flow.

I do not log in to your account, do not collect passwords/API keys/cookies, and do not handle payment/KYC/wallet/tax operations. I only review desensitized screenshots, error text, and sample input/output, then deliver a Chinese diagnosis report.

## Daily 10-Minute Operating Loop

1. Check Xianyu chats.
2. Check Xiaohongshu comments and DMs.
3. Check WeChat replies only in places where service posts were allowed.
4. Route every qualified lead into the same safe intake:
   - platform name;
   - failed step;
   - desensitized screenshot;
   - error text;
   - sample input;
   - expected output.
5. If the material is simple, quote 299.
6. If they ask for direct repair, route to 999 repair-plan scope.
7. If it involves production risk, repeated sending, multiple workflows, private credentials, or payment/KYC/wallet/tax, stop and re-scope to 1999+ or decline.

## Reply Rules

Use:

可以，先不要发账号密码、API Key、Token 或 Cookie。你把失败节点截图、报错文字、一条脱敏输入样例、期望输出、使用平台发来，我先判断适合 299 诊断、999 修复方案，还是需要重新拆复杂范围。

Do not use:

- 我保证修好;
- 我可以直接登录你后台;
- 发我账号密码;
- 发我 API Key;
- 我帮你绕过平台限制;
- 我先免费给你看完;
- 做完再付款.

## Stop Conditions

Stop replying or ask the user to handle manually when a lead asks for:

- account password;
- API Key, Token, Cookie, private repo access, or production credentials;
- payout, KYC, tax, wallet, or payment operations;
- bypassing a platform limit, captcha, risk control, or account lock;
- work that requires scraping private data or contacting third parties outside the platform;
- pay-after-work without platform protection.

## Weekly Decision

After 7 days, evaluate:

- Xianyu views, favorites, chats, paid orders.
- Xiaohongshu views, saves, comments, DMs.
- WeChat group replies and qualified screenshots.
- Number of leads that reached intake.
- Number of leads quoted 299 / 999 / 1999+.
- Number of paid orders.

If there are views but no chats:

Improve title and first image.

If there are chats but no payment:

Use `docs/CHINESE_BUYER_OBJECTION_HANDLING_PACK.md` and simplify the 299 offer.

If buyers keep asking for direct repair:

Lead with the 999 repair-plan offer instead of the 299 diagnosis offer.

If there is no exposure:

Change platform mix before changing the product. Prioritize Xianyu + Xiaohongshu first.
