# Chinese 299 To 999 Upsell Handoff

Use this after delivering a RMB 299 AI automation diagnosis report. The goal is to turn a clear diagnosis into a safe, scoped RMB 999 repair-plan offer without pressuring the buyer or expanding into risky account operation.

Do not ask for passwords, API keys, tokens, cookies, private repositories, production admin access, KYC, tax, payment, wallet, or platform-bypass work.

## When To Offer 999

Offer a 999 repair-plan upgrade only when the 299 diagnosis identifies a concrete repair path.

Good upgrade signals:

- One failed workflow or scenario is clearly involved.
- One or two nodes are the likely cause.
- The fix is likely field mapping, JSON path, Webhook payload shape, prompt output schema, retry setting, environment variable naming, or a small script change.
- The buyer wants exact repair steps, not just diagnosis.
- The buyer can test the fix themselves after receiving instructions.
- No login, password, API Key, Token, Cookie, wallet, KYC, payment, or private customer data is needed.

Do not offer 999 when:

- The buyer wants direct login operation.
- Multiple platforms or multiple workflows are failing.
- The buyer expects full development or long-term maintenance.
- The issue requires production credentials or private customer data.
- The buyer asks for platform bypass, scraping evasion, wallet, payment, KYC, or identity handling.

## Post-Diagnosis Message

诊断报告已经整理完。你可以先按报告里的“优先修复顺序”和“最小测试步骤”自己检查。

如果你希望继续往前推进，我建议下一步不要直接大改，而是做一个 999 元的小范围修复方案：

- 把最可能出问题的节点逐一拆开。
- 写清楚字段/JSON/Webhook/API/提示词应该怎么改。
- 给你一套最小测试步骤。
- 写明如果失败应该回滚到哪里。
- 标出哪些地方不能碰账号、密钥或生产数据。

这个 999 方案仍然不需要你给账号密码，也不需要 API Key。你可以按方案自己操作，或者后面再决定是否需要更大范围救援。

## Short Version

299 诊断已经定位到问题方向。下一步如果你想要具体修复路径，可以升级 999：我会给你字段/节点/API/JSON 的调整方案、测试步骤和回滚提醒。仍然不需要账号密码或密钥。

## If Buyer Hesitates

不急着升级也可以。你可以先按 299 报告里的第一优先级做一次最小测试。

如果测试后仍然失败，再升级 999 会更划算，因为到那时范围更清楚，不容易把钱花在不确定的大修上。

## If Buyer Wants Full Repair For 999

999 适合小范围修复方案，不适合完整代运营或多平台救援。

如果只是一个流程、一个失败点、一个接口或一个 JSON 映射问题，999 可以覆盖修复路径和测试步骤。

如果涉及多个平台、多条流程、多轮测试、重复调用、成本异常或生产级稳定性，需要按 1999+ 重新确认范围。

## If Buyer Wants You To Login

为了保护你的账号和数据，999 仍然不默认登录账号，也不收密码、API Key、Token 或 Cookie。

我可以把修复步骤写到你能自己执行的程度。如果后续确实需要协作操作，也必须重新确认安全边界，不能直接发敏感权限。

## 999 Repair-Plan Deliverables

A 999 repair-plan delivery should include:

1. Problem summary.
2. Confirmed likely root cause.
3. Exact field/node/API/JSON/prompt changes to try.
4. Minimal test payload or test case.
5. Pass/fail checklist.
6. Rollback note.
7. Risk warning.
8. What requires a larger 1999+ rescue scope.

## After Buyer Accepts 999

收到。这个 999 范围我会按“小范围修复方案”来交付，不做账号登录、不碰密钥、不处理付款/KYC/钱包/平台风控。

我会基于你已经提供的脱敏材料，把修复步骤、测试步骤和回滚提醒整理出来。如果中途发现范围超过单点修复，我会先停下来说明原因，不会默认扩大范围。

## Success Definition

The upsell is successful if the buyer clearly agrees to one of these:

- they want exact repair steps after the 299 diagnosis;
- they want field/node/API/JSON/prompt changes written out;
- they want a test and rollback checklist;
- they accept that 999 is not full platform operation or account login.
