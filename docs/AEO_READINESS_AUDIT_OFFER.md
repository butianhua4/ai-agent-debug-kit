# AEO Readiness Audit Offer

## Positioning

Help founders and small teams understand whether their public website, product page, or service listing is easy for AI search engines and answer engines to understand.

This is not traditional SEO. The focus is answer engine optimization: whether ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity-style search, AI overviews, and agentic buying flows can quickly identify:

- what the product does
- who it is for
- what problem it solves
- proof that it is real
- pricing or service scope
- trust and safety signals
- clear next actions

## English Listing Title

I will audit your website for AI search and answer engine readiness
