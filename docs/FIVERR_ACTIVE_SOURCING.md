## Active-Sourcing Rule

When Fiverr Briefs is 0, do not keep waiting inside Fiverr.

Move immediately to:

1. public bounty screening
2. public GitHub issues
3. productized service assets
4. delivery templates and report automation
5. user-approved new gig preparation

## Do Not Do

- Do not open external buyer links.
- Do not treat a message as a paid order.
- Do not move payment or communication off Fiverr.
- Do not handle payout, KYC, tax, subscription, promotion, wallet, or high-permission authorization.
